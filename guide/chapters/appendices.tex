\section{Result Codes}

\begin{reftable}{lp{0.43\linewidth}p{0.42\linewidth}}
0  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_OK} & success \\
1  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_NO\_DEVICE} & command-interface signature absent \\
2  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_TIMEOUT} & polling budget exhausted \\
3  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_PROTOCOL} & malformed or inconsistent transport reply \\
4  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_NOT\_SUPPORTED} & target, command or direct hardware unavailable \\
5  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_INVALID\_ARGUMENT} & caller supplied an invalid argument \\
6  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_IO} & filesystem, network or transfer failure \\
7  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_DEVICE} & target reported a failure \\
8  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_TRUNCATED} & retained buffer did not hold the whole reply \\
9  & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_ERR\_ABORTED} & exchange was abandoned \\
10 & \cmd{ULTIMATE\_END} & directory or socket stream ended normally \\
\end{reftable}

\cmd{ultimate\_strerror()} returns a printable description.
\cmd{ultimate\_device\_code()} returns the last target's raw numeric status for
diagnostics; its meaning depends on the target.
